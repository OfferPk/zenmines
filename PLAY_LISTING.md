## Full Description (<=4000 characters)

Rediscover the timeless satisfaction of Minesweeper in a refined, tranquil experience designed for peace of mind and pure logic.

ZenMines transforms the classic logic puzzle into a serene journey. With clean aesthetics, procedural ambient audio feedback, and a guaranteed safe first tap, every game is a pure test of your deductive skill with zero frustrations.

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES

• 100% Safe First Tap: Your very first move is always guaranteed safe and opens up an expanse, giving you immediate tactical clues to begin solving.

• Multiple Grids & Difficulties:
  - Beginner (9×9, 10 Mines) — Quick, relaxing coffee break puzzles.
  - Intermediate (16×16, 40 Mines) — Balanced strategic deduction.
  - Expert (16×30, 99 Mines) — Deep, expansive challenge for puzzle masters.

• Dual Play Controls:
  - Quick Mode Toggle: Seamlessly swap between Dig ⛏️ and Flag 🚩 modes with a single thumb tap.
  - Smart Long-Press: Hold any unopened cell to immediately place or remove flags.
  - Smart Chording: Tap any revealed number whose flags match its count to instantly clear surrounding safe tiles.

• Smart Hint Assistant: Stuck on a tricky corner? Use the gentle Hint tool to deduce logical next moves without spoiling the fun.

• Procedural Zen Audio & Haptics:
  - Crisp, musical chimes and soft clicks synthesized on-device via Web Audio.
  - Satisfying gentle haptic vibrations for each dig, flag, and victory cascade.
  - Instant mute toggle for quiet settings.

• Themes to Match Your Mood:
  - Zen Dark: Calming deep slate with soft cyan accents.
  - Paper Light: Clean minimalist stone and warm charcoal.
  - Neon Pulse: High-contrast vibrant glowing grid.

• Personal Statistics & Records:
  - Track games played, win rate percentage, and streaks.
  - Record your all-time best completion times for each difficulty level.

• 100% Offline & Private:
  - Zero internet connection required. Play on flights, commutes, or off-grid retreats.
  - No ads, no in-app purchases, no account sign-in, and zero data tracking.

Challenge your mind and unwind with ZenMines today!
