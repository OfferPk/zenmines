How to play ZenMines

  • First tap is safe. Your opening move always avoids mines and clears some space.
  • Read the numbers. Each one counts the mines touching that square.
  • Mark your hunches. Right-click or switch to Flag mode to place a flag.
  • Move faster. Tap a number with the matching flags to reveal its neighbours.
  • Long-press a square to do the opposite of the current Dig/Flag mode.
  • Need a breather? Tap Pause — the clock stops and the board is hidden.
  • Journey mode has 24 stages that grow bigger and denser. Clear one to unlock the next.
  • Play anywhere. ZenMines works offline once loaded, and your unfinished board is saved automatically.
